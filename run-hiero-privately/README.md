# How to read the Phase 1 documents

The package has two main documents: the Executive Summary for decision makers, and the Gap Analysis for the people who plan and do the work. The other documents add detail for readers who want it.

- __Executive Summary:__ The only document one needs to read to understand the exercise and its outcomes. It was written to be enough on its own.
- __Gap Analysis and the gap register:__ These are aimed at anyone who will plan or work on closing the gaps. They are also the natural next read for anyone who wants to go beyond the Executive Summary and better understand the possible next steps.
- __Technical Assessment:__ This document gives our impression of the Hiero ecosystem and how ready it is for running a private production network. It also offers a more balanced view than the Gap Analysis, which focuses only on the missing pieces. Readers who do not need that view can skip it.
- __Deployment Validation Report:__ This document summarizes what we did and which problems we had to solve along the way. Readers who do not need that detail can skip it.
- __Hiero Private Network Runbook and Test Report:__ The runbook contains the step-by-step procedure and scripts we used to deploy a private Hiero network, and the test report contains the full test results for it. They are aimed at engineers who want to verify our steps or reproduce our results.
